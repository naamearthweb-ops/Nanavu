About Nanavu

NANAVU is a national-level Sustainable
Conclave bringing together education,
industry, research, practice, and policy to
turn sustainability into real-world action.
Aligned with Kerala’s carbon-neutral
vision and India’s Net Zero 2070 goal,
NANAVU explores practical, affordable,
and accessible sustainable construction
through discussions, innovation, and
collaboration.

About Sustainability Council - TKMCE

The Campus Sustainability Council
(CSC), TKMCE Kollam, is a crossdisciplinary body of faculty, students,
and industry experts working to embed
sustainability into campus learning,
practices, and systems.
Through student engagement, green
campus initiatives, experiential learning,
innovation, research, and industry
collaboration, the CSC aims to nurture
responsible citizens who think, practise,
and carry sustainability forward.

NAVANU is a sustainability-focused conclave that brings
together students, architects, engineers, industry
professionals, organisations, and sustainability practitioners
to explore the future of the built environment. The conclave
aims to move beyond sustainability as a theoretical concept
by creating a platform for knowledge exchange, practical
learning, innovation, and collaboration in sustainable
construction.
