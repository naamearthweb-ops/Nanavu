About NANAVU
NANAVU is a sustainability-focused conclave
that brings together students, architects,
engineers, industry professionals,
organisations, and sustainability practitioners
to explore the future of the built environment.
The conclude aims to move beyond
sustainability as a theoretical concept by
creating a platform for knowledge exchange,
practical learning, innovation, and
collaboration in sustainable construction.

About the Campus Sustainability
Council (CSC) – TKMCE

What Happens at
NANAVU

The Campus Sustainability Council (CSC)
brings together the students, families, and
educators. Their aim is to provide a platform
for collaboration, education, and innovation.
It serves as a platform for collaboration,
awarding, and action towards a greener and
more responsible campus.

Nanavu empowers the next
generation of architects, engineers,
educators, researchers, and
practitioners through experiential
learning, research, workshops, and
collaborative projects focused on
sustainable building technologies
and materials.

About Naamearth
Naamearth is a sustainability-driven
initiative dedicated to advancing natural
regenerative, and responsible approaches
to sustainability. It combines traditional
knowledge, contemporary engineering,
research, and hands-on practice to explore
solutions that are ecologically, affordable,
resilient, and relevant to the future of
construction.
