ABOUT SUSTAINABILITY COUNCIL - TKMCE
The Campus Sustainability Council (CSC), TKMCE Kollam, is an
cross-disciplinary body comprising faculty, students, and
industry experts, established to drive sustainability
transformation across the campus. It focuses on integrating
sustainability into learning, practices, and campus systems
through student engagement, green campus initiatives,
sustainability audits, experiential learning, innovation,
research, and industry collaboration. Ultimately, CSC aims to
nurture responsible citizens who think, practise, innovate, and
carry sustainability forward

ABOUT NANAVU
NANAVU - Nurturing the Futures is a national-level sustainable
conclave exploring how sustainable and natural construction
can become a practical, affordable, accessible and rewarding
pathway for the next generation of professionals. It brings
together education, industry, research, practice and policy to
explore materials, technologies, practices and career
opportunities in sustainable construction. To nurture a future
where sustainable construction is accessible, practical, valued
and a rewarding professional pathway.

ABOUT NAAMEARTH
Naamearth is a sustainability-driven initiative dedicated to advancing
natural, regenerative, and responsible approaches to the built environment.
It brings together traditional knowledge, contemporary engineering,
research, and hands-on practice to explore solutions that are ecological,
affordable, resilient, and relevant to the future of construction.
Naamearth seeks to empower the next generation of architects, engineers,
educators, researchers, and practitioners with the knowledge, skills, and
experience needed to transform the way we design and build. Through
experiential learning, research, workshops, and collaborative projects, it
creates opportunities to move beyond conventional practices and engage
directly with sustainable building technologies and materials.
